Architecture

One organism, many nerves

Independent engines trade their own markets through isolated broker bridges, coordinated by a macro brain that allocates risk by regime — never amputating a market, always weighting toward the validated edge.

▣ Market Engines

Dedicated engines for gold scalping & swing, crypto, FX and equities — each with its own management mechanics (grid add-ons, break-even, trailing, time-exit).

◈ Macro Brain

A regime/sentiment layer publishes risk-on/off bias and volatility-aware sizing consumed by every engine — a meta-allocator that follows the regime, not the hype.

⇆ Broker Bridges

Containerized MT5/REST bridges per account, fully isolated, with a reconciliation layer that treats the broker as the single source of truth.

✦ LLM Committee

Language models interpret news, sentiment and free-text signals — turning unstructured input into structured, pondered trading decisions.

⟲ Management-Aware Backtester

A replay engine that models the actual management (add-ons, BE, trailing, costs) — validating the edge out-of-sample before a single euro is scaled.

🛰 Live Signal Relay

External signals captured and executed in seconds via a low-latency automation chain — with anti-stale and anti-duplicate guards.

Doctrine

Rigid rules, measured truth

Principles distilled from hundreds of audits — the philosophy that keeps the system honest.

The edge is in the management.Entries are commodities; the durable advantage lives in how a position is grown, protected and exited.
